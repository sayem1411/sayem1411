# Hi, I am Abdul Kaium Sayem

Ph.D. Candidate in Metallurgical Engineering at the University of Utah (expected May 2027), based in Salt Lake City, UT.

My research is on graphite recovery from lithium-ion battery black mass via froth flotation, mineral processing, and critical materials extraction.

## Research focus
- Graphite and cathode separation from Li-ion black mass: mechanical pretreatment, binder removal, particle size control, froth flotation
- Gallium recovery from mine waste by reverse flotation
- Copper and rare earth recovery: leaching, solvent extraction, designed experiments

## Selected results

- 96.20% graphite grade in pretreatment work
